About

Diforma Group

With more than 20 years of global experience, Diforma Group is a trusted strategic partner for leading FMCG brands seeking innovative merchandising solutions that drive results at the point of sale.

We specialize in designing and manufacturing custom retail displays and custom pop display solutions that help brands capture consumer attention during the critical First Moment of Truth (FMOT). Our displays are created to strengthen brand visibility, enhance the shopping experience, and encourage purchase decisions when they matter most.

From global corporations to growing businesses, Diforma Group works closely with companies of every size to develop high-impact merchandising strategies tailored to their products, retail environments, and marketing objectives. By combining creativity, manufacturing expertise, and market insight, we deliver display solutions that improve product visibility, increase sales rotation, and create memorable in-store brand experiences.

Our mission is to transform retail spaces into powerful sales opportunities through innovative, high-quality display solutions that connect brands with shoppers and maximize commercial performance.
